Return and Refund Policy

1. Customers may return products within 30 days of delivery.

2. Products must be unused and in their original packaging.

3. Customers must provide an order number to request a return.

4. Refunds will be processed within 5-7 business days after the returned product is received.

5. Shipping fees are non-refundable unless the product is defective.

6. Defective products can be returned without additional shipping charges.

7. To request a return, contact customer support with your order number and reason for return.
